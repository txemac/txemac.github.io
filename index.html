Handling Negation in Classification of Clinical Texts

Jacinto Mata · Manuel J. Maña · Txema Bermúdez · Noa P. Cruz · Patricia JiménezClinical NLP · i2b2 workshop

Proceedings of the Workshop on Challenges in Natural Language Processing for Clinical Data (November 2008). The work studies negation handling in clinical narratives and the use of controlled vocabularies to reconcile different expressions of the same medical concept.

  • NegEx and regular expressions for detecting negation.
  • Evaluation with repeated 3-fold cross-validation; the paper discusses weaker results on the test set and possible overfitting.
